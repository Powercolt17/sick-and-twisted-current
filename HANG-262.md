# Hang Em High — complete bounty progression

The original full-column Outlaw Wild remains the feature artwork. Rejected gallows/reel-drop experiments are not restored. Existing background and full-column entrance stay in place.

Each permanent Outlaw earns one stamp per contributing winning tumble, independent of ways/cell count. Three stamps double its multiplier, up to 64x. Surplus stamps carry. The winning board pays at its old multiplier; the next board uses the upgrade. Capturing three permanent Outlaws unlocks The Last Sentence: two stamps per contributing win and an opportunity for a temporary fourth 8x Outlaw on later spins. The temporary Outlaw survives that spin's tumbles, gains no stamps and leaves before another spin. Existing capture upgrades, additive multipliers, +3 scatter retriggers, 24-spin maximum and overall payout cap remain.

Presentation: stamped meters below each permanent reel, synchronized multiplier reveals, distinct Last Sentence/fourth-Outlaw notices, one-spin labeling and departure fade. Existing stamp, tension and impact samples are cued by the pausable presentation clock. Small stamp awards have quieter sound without camera shake. Large changes receive the heavier impact. The wood receipt collects the actual already-paid cents into Outlaw bounty shares; shared wins are divided evenly among contributing Outlaws. This allocation never creates another award. Live total, zero reward, keyboard continuation, pause, mute and reduced motion are retained.

Fixed distribution is still calibrated to the existing 96.4% demo-credit feature target, with no player-history input. Catalog contains 12,000 complete deterministic paths. Current weighted median is 77.27% of buy cost, retrigger probability 19.32%, three-lock probability 23.81%. These are model statistics, not per-play promises. Catalog source and reproducible baker are included. The paused catalog was validated end to end before integration.

Bug fixes found during development: immutable stamp reward snapshots (later captures must not mutate an earlier reward); correct spawn/display metadata on stamp upgrades; locked-reel retention through upgrades (no second drop); MAX/big-win accounting remains separate; temporary removal before the next spin.

Verification: 31 Node test files pass. All 12,000 catalog paths match the resolver. Additional tests execute the real runTumbles function with deterministic outcomes, checking single crediting, receipt cents, retriggers, stamp timing, one finale and temporary arrival/departure. Final scripted QA uses seed 4178: 15 spins, $6,560.00 at a $1 base bet. This deliberately illustrative path is only for the debug walkthrough; purchases and normal feature starts remain random. Physical phone/Safari and subjective speaker balance are not certified by these checks.

No new paid media was generated for this feature. Two user-requested border concepts are a separate preview deliverable and are not included in this release.
